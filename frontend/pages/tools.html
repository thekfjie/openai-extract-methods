CF Cookie 抓取

  • 代码：/opt/automyai/tools/cf_cookie
  • 数据：/opt/automyai/data/cf_cookie
  • 主入口：cf.unified-attack.js / cf.auto-attack.js
  • 代理：/opt/automyai/data/cf_cookie/proxies/proxies.txt
